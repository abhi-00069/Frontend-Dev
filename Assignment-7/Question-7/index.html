The Art of Good Typography

Typography is the visual component of the written word. It plays a crucial role in how people perceive and read text. The way a font looks can dramatically influence readability and emotion. In digital design, typography defines not only how text looks but also how it feels.

When choosing a font, it’s essential to consider both functionality and style. A bold headline font grabs attention, while a clean body font supports effortless reading. Notice how different font weights and spacing improve balance and rhythm across a page.

Good typography is invisible — it enhances content without drawing attention to itself. Using proper line height, letter spacing, and alignment ensures text is comfortable to read and visually harmonious.
